    Your own 3D models

    Upload a .glb, .gltf (with embedded data) or .vrm, up to 40 MB. A head model replaces your head; a full avatar (VRM / VRoid, or any rig with neck and head bones) is cut at the neck so only its head shows. It follows your head, and your face too when it has VRM expressions or morph targets named like ARKit blendshapes (eyeBlinkLeft, jawOpen, mouthSmileLeft…). Only you can see your models.

    Free models to try: VRoid Hub (anime avatars, .vrm; download the ones whose author allows it) · VRoid Studio (make your own, free) · Sketchfab: ARKit faces and animal heads (download as glTF / .glb) · facecap.glb (a face with all 52 ARKit shapes) · a sample VRM avatar. Check each model's license.
