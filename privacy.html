Privacy Policy

Effective 24 November 2026

pixie (pixie.skapi.com) is a free app for painting and sharing 16×16 pixel art. It is run by an independent developer ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information pixie collects, why, and what you can do about it.

The short version

  • To make an account we ask for only your name and email address. Guests give neither.
  • We don't track you, show ads, use analytics, or sell or share your information.
  • What you post (pixies, captions, tags, comments, likes, follows and your avatar) is visible to other signed-in users, unless you share a pixie as private.
  • You can delete your pixies at any time, and delete your whole account from your profile.

1. What we collect

Your account

  • Name and email address, when you sign up. Your email address is used to confirm your account and reset your password. It is never shown to other users.
  • Password. It is handled by our backend provider (see section 4); we can't see it.
  • If you continue with Google, pixie keeps only your email address and name from your Google account. If your Google account has no name, pixie uses the part of your email address before the "@" as your name; you can change it in your profile. (Google also sends your Google account number and a profile picture link; pixie doesn't use them.) We never see your Google password, and we get no access to anything else in your Google account.
  • Guest accounts have no name, email address or password. A guest account and everything it posted is deleted 30 minutes after the guest leaves, or right away when they log out.

What you post

  • Your pixies (the pixel art itself, stored as 256 color values), captions, tags, comments, likes, the people you follow, and your avatar.
  • The time each of these was posted, and the account identifier our backend gives your account, so posts can be shown with their author.

Notifications (only if you turn them on)

  • A push subscription address made by your browser, so we can send likes, comments, remixes, new followers and new pixies from people you follow to this device. Turning notifications off, or logging out, removes it.

What we don't collect

  • No location, contacts, camera or microphone.
  • When you turn a picture into pixels, the picture is processed on your device and never uploaded. Only the 16×16 result is shared, and only if you share it.
  • No analytics, advertising identifiers, tracking cookies or fingerprinting.

2. How we use it

Only to run pixie: to create and secure your account, show your name with your pixies and comments, send account emails (sign-up confirmation and password reset), and deliver the notifications you turned on. We never use your information for advertising, and we never sell it.

3. Who can see what

  • Signed-in users (including guests) can see your name, avatar, public pixies, captions, tags, comments, likes, remixes, and how many followers you have and how many people you follow.
  • Private pixies can be seen only by you.
  • Your email address is never shown to anyone.
  • Other users can download public pixies as images and remix them (see the Terms of Service).
  • When you share a link to a public pixie, pixie makes a public preview of it (the picture, its caption and your name), so the site you post the link on can show it. It is deleted when you delete the pixie or your account.

4. Service providers

  • Skapi runs pixie's backend: accounts, the database, account emails, notifications and website hosting. Your information is stored on its servers in the Asia Pacific (Seoul) region.
  • Browser push services (from Google, Apple or Mozilla, depending on your browser) deliver notifications if you turn them on.
  • Google Fonts and the jsDelivr content network deliver pixie's fonts and a code library. Like any website you load files from, they receive your IP address and browser details.

5. Storage on your device

pixie keeps a few things in your browser's local storage: your login session, the drawing you're working on, your last color, your activity list and whether you dismissed a banner. Its offline cache (a service worker) keeps the app's files so it opens quickly. None of it is used for tracking. Clearing this site's data in your browser removes all of it.

6. How long we keep it, and deleting it

  • Your pixies, comments and likes stay until you delete them. You can delete any pixie with its trash button.
  • Delete my account (in your profile) deletes your pixies, comments, likes and avatar right away and closes your account. The rest of your account, including your name and email address, is deleted after 90 days. Until then you can get it back by logging in again.
  • Guest accounts are deleted 30 minutes after the guest leaves, or right away when they log out.
  • If you remix someone's pixie, its maker can remove your remix from the remix family (see the Terms of Service). When they do, your remix records that they made that change, and anyone who can see the remix can see who made it.

7. Your choices and rights

You can see and change your name in your profile, delete anything you posted, turn notifications off, and delete your account at any time. Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to ask what information we hold about you, to correct it, or to have it deleted. Contact us (section 11) and we'll help.

8. Children

pixie is not meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

9. Where your information is processed

Your information is stored by Skapi in the Asia Pacific (Seoul) region and may be processed in other countries where our providers operate. By using pixie you understand that it may be handled outside your country.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we'll update this page and its effective date. If a change is significant, we'll also let you know in the app.

11. Contact

Questions or requests: open an issue at github.com/broadwayinc/pixie/issues. Issues there are public, so please don't include your email address or other personal details. To delete your account, use Delete my account in your profile.
